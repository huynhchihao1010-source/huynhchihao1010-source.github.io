03
My brand · Marketplace simulation · MKTG8080

FORME: a premium carbon-fibre bike brand that finished #1.

I ran FORME's strategy in a competitive marketplace simulation, Speed riders first and Mountain second. The defining call was to open seven cities in one quarter and accept a one-off loss while rivals held four stores or fewer. Then I pruned the weak price tiers and put about 70% of the investment pool into R&D. FORME closed first in total performance, quarterly and cumulative, with $10.3M net profit in Q6.

CH.2 · PACE industry project · SGK

NewCo Proposal: from packaging supplier to experience partner

A Blue Ocean Strategy proposal for SGK. We repositioned the business across the whole consumer journey with two connected innovations. ShelfEdge puts interactive QR packaging and pop-up activations at the shelf. SmartPost turns recycling after use into a gamified ESG loyalty loop with traceable dashboards. Built on the industry map, strategy canvas, ERRC grid, buyer utility map, three tiers of non-customers, six paths and price corridor. I led the team of five and compiled the 10,000-word executive report.

  • A$109,450 ShelfEdge
  • A$127,450 SmartPost
  • ≥ 50K QR scans in 3 mo
  • < $1.20 per engagement
  • ≥ 10K ESG dashboard views
